## About the source activity (please read)
- The course notes give: the goal (**leave the module with a short, personal, realistic action plan**), the instruction (**write 3 small, realistic things you will do in the next 30 days to use AI at work — small, not “become an AI expert”**), the three examples (weekly status email · AI tool for meeting notes · summarize a long document once a week), about 3 minutes of writing, 2–3 volunteers sharing one step, two debrief questions (*Which of your 3 steps is the easiest to start tomorrow? / What is one thing that could stop you — and how can you plan around it?*) and the tip to keep a photo of the plan. The worksheet handout is **not in the deck**.
- **The central requirement is preserved: three practical, achievable commitments within 30 days.** The 7 / 14 / 30-day windows, the destinations, tool and evidence choices and the three obstacles are **planning scaffolds written for this game**, not part of the original worksheet. The “manager conversation starter” follows a later course slide (what I will do / what I will not do / how you will know).
- It is not a 30-question game: the core is three commitments; the rest is lightweight.

## The journey (five stages)
1. **Choose your destination** — save time · improve communication · organize work · improve research · strengthen decision support · custom goal (your own words are required for a custom goal). Choosing one lights up the start of the path and the “future me” beacon on the animated map.
2. **Build your three milestones** — stations for Days 1–7, 8–14 and 15–30 (suggestions, not deadlines). For each: **what you will do** (5+ words, in your own words — the course examples and examples for your destination are offered, but an example copied word for word does *not* count until you make it yours), **which AI tool or approach** (only tools your company allows, or describe your own), **when** (a target day on a mini calendar) and **what evidence will show it happened**. A completed station lights its node and a section of the path. Everything stays editable — also from the progress rail.
3. **Obstacle challenge** — three barriers: not enough time · unreliable AI output · workplace data restrictions. Pick up to two responses each (some sound practical but are risky, e.g. pasting a real document into a public AI tool), add your own contingency, and see how each safeguard keeps your plan moving. The game never encourages uploading confidential workplace information to public AI tools.
4. **Roadmap check** — a structural review (an action, a tool, a day, evidence — 12 parts) with friendly “how to fix it” tips and a **Revise** button. It states plainly that it only confirms the parts exist; you rate each step’s realism honestly (easy / a stretch / too big, with advice to shrink anything too big). No keyword matching is presented as proof of quality.
5. **Future me** — an animated completion, your name and roadmap (clearly marked **PLANNED — nothing has happened yet**), a **future-self message assembled from your own plan with simple templates (not a prediction)**, your “why”, the step you could start tomorrow, an optional start date, and: **Copy Roadmap · Download Personal Action Plan (.txt) · Print-friendly plan · Reminder text (copy) · Calendar file (.ics) · Manager conversation (copy)**.

## Scoring (transparent, max 91 — structure and thoughtfulness, never speed)
| Part | Points |
|---|---|
| Destination chosen | 5 |
| Your own goal in your own words (4+ words) | 3 |
| Three commitments (each: action 4 · tool 2 · day 2 · evidence 2) | 30 |
| Obstacles (per barrier: safe response 4 each, up to 2; −4 for a risky one; own contingency 6+ words 4) | 36 |
| Honest realism check of each step | 6 |
| Revised something after the roadmap check | 5 |
| Your “why” (4+ words) | 4 |
| Picked the step to start tomorrow | 2 |
Coins: 250 at start, hint = 50 (one per stage, guidance only). Coins break ties. Ranks: ≥72 Journey Leader · ≥52 Path Builder · ≥30 Explorer · else First Step.

About **8 messages per player** (more if someone revisits and edits).

## Privacy and honest limits
- The trainer must be able to read the commitments, so they **are** sent over the **public ntfy relay** (sanitized: emails, links and long digit strings are removed; length-limited). The game tells participants to write small, realistic actions and **no confidential workplace details**. The `2026` PIN only hides the interface; the relay is not private.
- The roadmap check only confirms structure. It cannot tell whether a plan is realistic or good, and the game says so. The future-self message is a fill-in template, not a prediction.
- **Not yet tested on real ntfy.sh or across real devices.** Tested in a headless browser against a local relay that imitates ntfy (SSE, polling, storage blocked, Arabic, an emulated touch phone using taps, file downloads, clipboard, and message sizes under 4096 bytes; 73 checks). The print dialog itself cannot be automated: the print layout is built and the print call is verified, but please try **Print-friendly plan** once in a real browser. Run the two-device checklist before a live class. ntfy.sh free limits are per IP (~250 publishes/day): ~30 players on one network is a safe size.

## Two-device test checklist
1. Laptop: published link → Trainer → PIN 2026. Note the 5-character code and `● LIVE`.
2. Phone: open the player link (or type the code) → name on the journey pass → **Start my journey** (the map lights up). The privacy advice is visible on the landing page.
3. Laptop: the traveller appears in seconds. Phone: choose a destination (the path start glows) and continue.
4. Phone: build the three stations — try an example copied as is (it does not count), then personalize it; pick a tool, a day and evidence. Each completed station lights its section of the path.
5. Phone: obstacles — try one risky response and read the feedback; add a contingency. Roadmap check: use **Revise**, rate the steps. Laptop: the table shows milestones with days, shield/⚠ cells and plan checks; expand the row to read the three commitments.
6. Phone: future me — add the “why”, pick a first step, set a start date; **Copy**, **Download .txt**, **.ics**, **Print-friendly plan**. Check the dates in the reminders.
7. Laptop: **Reveal leaderboard** → opens by itself on the phone; **Hide** closes it. Projector view shows the same.
8. Switch language on both; check RTL and that nothing is cut off on the phone.
9. Refresh the phone mid-journey → **Resume the journey** continues. Laptop: **Reset session** → new code. Check Sound ON/OFF and “reduce motion”.
